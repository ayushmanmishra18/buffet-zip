===============================
Date: 2026-10-06 | Code: github.com/ayushmanmishra18/buffet (main @ be3a618+)

1) UPLOAD
- Upload the contents of Godwin(buffet)/Main/ into the site's public root
  (or the folder your domain points to) via FileZilla. Overwrite all.
- DO NOT upload a .env file. The zip has none. Keep the server's existing .env.
- Server .env must have: APP_ENV=production, APP_DEBUG=false,
  correct DB_* credentials, APP_URL=https://www.buffetlounge.in

2) PHP REQUIREMENTS (cPanel Select PHP Version / server)
- PHP 8.1 or 8.2 with extensions: curl, fileinfo, gd, mbstring,
  openssl, pdo_mysql, sodium, zip, imagick (imagick = QR codes on restaurant pages)

3) AFTER UPLOAD (cPanel Terminal, or ask host support)
- php artisan storage:link        (recreates public/storage images link)
- php artisan config:clear
- php artisan view:clear
- php artisan permission:cache-reset
- Ensure storage/ and bootstrap/cache/ are writable (775)
